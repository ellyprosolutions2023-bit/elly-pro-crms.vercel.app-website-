Everything the business needs, nothing it does not

Modules switch on per business. Start with the till and add rentals or accounting when you are ready.

🛒Sales and stock

For shops, hardware and building materials, hotels and restaurants.

  • New sale screen with shifts, cash counts and over/short tracking
  • Cash, M-Pesa, card and credit sales, with receivables and ageing
  • Returns and credit notes
  • Products, categories, stock movements, transfers between branches and adjustments
  • Reorder alerts, requisitions, purchase orders and goods received
  • Stock valuation and warehouse availability

🏘️Rentals

For landlords and property managers.

  • Units, tenants and leases, with documents attached
  • Monthly rent charges generated for you, plus water, service charge and other charges
  • Receipts numbered in your own format
  • Arrears list
  • VAT on commercial rent or Monthly Rental Income tax
  • Tax withheld by tenants recorded against the rent, with certificate numbers

📒Accounting

Built so an accountant is comfortable in it, and an owner can still read it.

  • Chart of accounts with current and non-current assets and liabilities
  • Sales, purchases, rent and payments post to the books automatically
  • Journals, cash books and month locking
  • Pay bills and write cheques, printed on your bank's leaf, with approval above a limit
  • Bank statement import with auto-matching for five banks
  • Bank reconciliation and a daily reconciliation report
  • Bank Position report: balances, overdraft limits, uncleared cheques and receivables
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, VAT, ageing, cash flow
  • Customer and supplier statements of account
  • Year-end close to retained earnings, reversible

🛡️Control and safety

Staff do their job. The owner stays in charge.

  • Separate businesses and branches, with people limited to their own
  • Roles for admin, manager, cashier and accountant
  • Audit log of who did what
  • Nothing is deleted from the books. Mistakes are reversed, and both entries stay visible
