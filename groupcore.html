What your group gets today

Members

  • Invitation only: nobody joins without an invite from an official
  • Each member accepts the privacy notice and gives consent on first sign-in
  • Suspend, reinstate or exit members, with a record of every change
  • Two-step login for officials

Meetings and notices

  • Schedule meetings with an agenda and let members reply yes, no or maybe
  • Record attendance and write minutes; adopted minutes are locked
  • Post notices and pin the important ones

Documents and records

  • Constitution with versions, and a record of who has read the current one
  • Upload PDF, Word, PNG or JPEG files, for example forms or photos of officials
  • Tamper-evident audit log you can check at any time

Coming next: contributions with M-Pesa (Paybill or Till), reminders by email and in the app, and group reports.

Built for trust

  • Each group sees only its own data. Other groups cannot see it.
  • Your group's logo and colours, so it feels like your own app.
  • Install it on a phone like an app, with no app store needed.
  • Designed around the Kenya Data Protection Act: your group decides, we only host.
  • Support by WhatsApp and email from Elly Pro Solutions.

Members' contributions and balances are visible to all members of the group, as your group decided.
