Privacy

Last updated October 2026. This summary covers this website. The Elly Pro CRMS application has its own Privacy Policy, shown when you create an account.

What this website collects

This site does not use accounts, advertising trackers or cookies for tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

The contact form

The form on the Contact page does not send your details to us directly. It prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. Only if you press send does WhatsApp deliver it to us. WhatsApp's own privacy terms then apply.

How we use what you send us

We use your name, business name and phone number to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a customer, to set up your account. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights
