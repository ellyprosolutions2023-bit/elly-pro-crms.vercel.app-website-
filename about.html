Built by an accountant who also writes software

Elly Pro Solutions is a business consultancy, accounting and tax practice, and software developer in Kenya.

Elly Pro CRMS began with a simple frustration: shops kept one set of records for the till, another for stock, and a third for the accountant. Month-end meant weeks of matching and chasing.

So the system is built the way an accountant would want the books kept. Every sale, rent receipt and payment posts to a proper ledger. Every bank line can be traced to a record. Nothing is deleted, because auditors and the KRA do not like gaps.

What we care about

  • Accuracy you can check. Totals agree to the ledger, and the screens say so.
  • Plain language. Screens talk about money in and money out, not jargon.
  • Local fit. M-Pesa, cheques, VAT, MRI, withholding tax and Kenyan banks are built in, not bolted on.
  • Real support. You reach us on WhatsApp.
