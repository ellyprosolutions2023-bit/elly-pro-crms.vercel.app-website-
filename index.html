Import the bank statement. The books do the matching.

Upload the PDF from DTB, KCB, NBK, Co-op or Absa. Elly Pro reads every line, checks the running balance, and ties each one to what you recorded.

  1. Upload the PDFIt checks opening and closing balances line by line before saving anything.
  2. Matches are found for youBy cheque number, M-Pesa code, and exact amount and date. Duplicates are skipped.
  3. You confirm the doubtful onesBook charges and deposits in a click. Anything booked can be undone.
  4. Reconcile and printDaily reconciliation and the Bank Position report print to PDF or Excel.

What the owner finally sees

  • Each bank account's balance against its overdraft limit
  • Cheques you have written but not yet released, and when they fall due
  • What customers owe, by the bank they usually pay into
  • Surplus or deficit across all accounts, ready to print

Picture PDFs from a scanner cannot be read. Use the PDF you download from internet banking.
