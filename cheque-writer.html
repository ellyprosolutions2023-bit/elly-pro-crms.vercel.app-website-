What it does

Writing

  • Amount in words worked out for you
  • Payee list, with corrections kept on record
  • Batch printing for many cheques at once

Printing

  • Prints through Windows, so it uses the printer you already have
  • Set up each bank's cheque layout once and line it up with a test print
  • Logo and company name supported

Records and control

  • Cheque register: written, printed, cancelled
  • Cheque books with leaf numbers
  • Optional sign-in for Administrator and Accountant users, with an activity log
  • Export to Excel and backup

Simple to own

  • One-time purchase. No monthly fee.
  • Your licence is tied to your computer, and checked offline.
  • Alicia, the built-in helper, answers how-to questions without internet.
  • Support by WhatsApp and email from Elly Pro Solutions.

Your cheque data stays on your computer. Sign-in keeps other people at the computer out of the program; it is not encryption, so keep the computer itself protected.
